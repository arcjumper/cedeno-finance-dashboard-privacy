Privacy Policy

Cedeno Finance Dashboard
Last updated: 2026-08-12

Summary

Cedeno Finance Dashboard is a personal finance application built and used by one person, its developer. It has no other users. It does not collect data about anyone else, and it does not share data with anyone.

Who this covers

The application has a single user, who is also its developer and operator. It is not offered to the public, has no registration or sign-up, and no other person can create an account or access it.

What data is accessed

Through the Plaid API, and only for accounts belonging to the operator:

  • Account balances and account metadata
  • Transaction history
  • Liabilities, including credit card, auto lease, and student loan details
  • Investment holdings

Financial institution login credentials are entered directly into Plaid Link and are never seen, received, or stored by this application. The application holds only Plaid access tokens, which permit read access to the accounts the operator has explicitly linked.

How it is used

To display balances and net worth, categorise and analyse spending, plan debt payoff, forecast cash flow, and prepare tax summaries. All of it for the operator’s own accounts.

The data is not used to train models offered to anyone else, is not aggregated with other people’s data, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

In a local SQLite database on the operator’s personal machine, protected by full-disk encryption and an operating system account secured with multi-factor authentication. The application is served on the loopback interface only and is not reachable from any network.

Who it is shared with

No one.

The data is not sold, rented, licensed, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. There are no analytics services, no advertising networks, no error reporting services, and no cloud backups. The only external service the application communicates with is the Plaid API, which is the source of the data.

Retention and deletion

Covered in the Data Retention and Deletion Policy. In short: transaction history is retained indefinitely, since the operator is the only data subject and long history is what the application is for; disconnecting an institution invalidates its access token at Plaid; deleting the local database removes everything, and can be done at any time.

Your rights

The only data subject is the operator, who holds the data directly and can inspect, export, or delete any of it at any time without making a request to any third party.
